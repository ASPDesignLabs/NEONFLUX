Invisible. Reactive. Ready when you are.

NEONFLUX is a combined Wear OS and Android experience that harnesses movement and haptic playback to assist with grounding and regulation.

It's built around two distinct decks, each serving a specific regulatory function:

  • Reactive Feedback [Reactor Deck]: for active stimming and grounding. It acts as an instrument, translating physical kinetic energy into sensory feedback. See Deck 02.
  • Rhythmic Entrainment [Clinical Deck]: for passive focus and regulation. It acts as a precise metronome for the nervous system. See Deck 03.

// Deck 01 (on the watch)

REACTOR

ACTIVE GROUNDING

REACTOR

CORE 72% 3h 45m
AUDIO: OFF

The default state on the watch. It turns movement into data you can feel — the CORE ring shown here is battery, not the haptic session itself.

How it works

  • The Loop: the watch monitors linear acceleration. When you move (shake, tilt, or flick), that energy is instantly translated into haptic vibration.
  • Stealth vs. Audio: by default, the system operates in STEALTH mode (haptics only). Tap AUDIO: OFF to toggle AUDIO: ON — movement will now generate a rising sci-fi synth tone proportional to your kinetic intensity.
  • The Mirror: the phone app's Bio-Metric Visualizer (see Mission Control) mirrors your movement in real time, closing the sensory loop between physical input and visual confirmation.

Haptic texture swapping

Double-tap anywhere on the background to hot-swap the feedback texture:

  • Pulse: standard linear feedback. Smooth and predictable.
  • Geiger: probabilistic clicking, denser as intensity rises. Excellent for fidgeting.
  • Throb: heavy, boosted impact (80ms). Best for deep pressure grounding.

Transitioning to Clinical

Rotate the digital crown (or physical bezel) to switch decks — a physical gesture instead of a tap, to prevent accidental mode switches during active use. You'll feel a mechanical click, then the interface slides to Clinical.

CLINICAL

PASSIVE REGULATION

CLINICAL

BPM60
INT50%

PROG: PULSE

INITIALIZE

While Reactor is about input, Clinical is about output — a precise, rhythmic haptic beat to help regulate breathing or maintain focus.

Configuration

Programmed from the phone — see Mission Control — before it ever reaches the watch:

  • Waveform: Pulse, Geiger, or Throb.
  • Frequency: BPM (beats per minute) — 60 BPM is standard for calming.
  • Intensity: motor strength.
  • Sleep Protocol: forces the watch screen and sensors off during the beat — for meditation or sleep without light pollution.

The sync event

  1. Tap UPLOAD CONFIGURATION on the phone.
  2. Phone animates SYNCHRONIZING...
  3. Watch displays a [ FIRMWARE UPDATING ] overlay.
  4. The watch loads the new settings and switches to Clinical automatically.

Engagement

Tap INITIALIZE on the watch. A 3-2-1 countdown initiates, then the haptic engine begins — an absolute-time drift correction algorithm keeps it from ever "dragging" or "rushing," staying perfectly on beat for hours.

// Safety & Lockdown

SAFEGUARD // LOCKDOWN_PROTOCOL

1. The Curtain (Focus Mode)

While Clinical Mode is running with Sleep Protocol enabled, a "Dark Curtain" fades in — saving OLED screens from burn-in and reducing visual overstimulation. Outside of Sleep Protocol, the deck UI stays up so it's always clear what's running. Use the Lockdown Gesture to stop the session (there's no separate "just peek" wake while Sleep Protocol is active).

2. Lockdown Gesture (Emergency Stop)

If sensation becomes overwhelming or you can't navigate the UI: place two fingers on the screen and hold for 3 seconds. Hard stop. Motors kill, audio cuts, and a confirmation buzz indicates the system is safe.

3. Safe Mode

To protect battery health, the app will refuse to run if the watch battery is below 15%.

4. Exit Dialog

Swiping "Back" triggers a confirmation before it lets you close the app:

TERMINATE? — the watch app's own exit dialog
  • Resume: return to the flux.
  • Halt: kill the app and return to the watch face.

// Initialization

INITIALIZATION

PHONE, THEN WATCH

1. Phone setup (Mission Control)

Launch NEONFLUX Mobile.

  • The Bio-Metric Visualizer will initially read AWAITING TELEMETRY — see Mission Control.
  • Ensure Bluetooth is active. No manual pairing is required; the app uses the Wearable Data Layer to automatically handshake with the nearest node.

2. Watch initialization (the node)

Launch NEONFLUX Wear.

  • You'll be greeted by the Reactor deck.
  • The central ring displays your CORE status — note that this differs from your device battery meter, as the haptic engine draws power differently depending on the active profile.

// Protocol Initiation

PROTOCOL INITIATION

MANUAL OVERRIDE REQUIRED

Status: not on the Play Store. To proceed, you must bypass Android's standard security protocols and sideload via ADB.
Security warning — read before proceeding.

By enabling "Developer Options" and "USB/Wireless Debugging," you are lowering your device's shields.

  • Sideloading risk: installing APKs from unknown sources can expose your device to malicious code. Only install files obtained directly from the official NeonFlux release page.
  • Open ports: ADB (Android Debug Bridge) allows external computers to send commands to your device. Always disable USB/Wireless Debugging when you're finished installing NeonFlux.
  • Warranty void: while rare, improper use of developer tools can destabilize your device. Proceed at your own risk.
